# Tide Of Tentacles Art Sources

These animation frames are adapted from ASCII works found in Christopher Johnson's ASCII Art Collection.

- Nautical category (ships and boats):
  - https://asciiart.website/cat.php?category_id=387
- Octopus entries (Carl Pilcher):
  - https://asciiart.website/art/562
  - https://asciiart.website/art/60
- Animals (Other) (Water) category (fish/sea life forms):
  - https://asciiart.website/cat.php?category_id=17

Adaptation notes:
- Converted static art into short 3 FPS frame loops.
- Kept each animation at 5 frames or fewer.
- Added per-line color tags for this engine's animated renderer.
